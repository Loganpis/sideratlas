NO.006 — THE PLANETARY NEBULA
@sideratlas

A Sun-like star expands into a giant
A dusty wind removes its outer envelope
The exposed core contracts and heats
Ultraviolet light ionizes the expelled gas
A faster wind compresses the growing nebula
Hydrogen, oxygen and nitrogen reveal different regions
A planetary nebula is not a supernova

CALCULATED
1 M☉ MIST/MESA track toward a ≈0.540 M☉ post-AGB core
Conserved wind cohorts and approximate radial pressure dynamics
Finite-photon hydrogen and approximate oxygen/nitrogen populations
PyNeb line emission, attenuation and approximate dust reprocessing
Spectral surface emission with ⟨T_local⁴⟩ = T_eff⁴

ASSUMPTIONS
Giant convection uses evolving statistical cells, not simulated fluid flow
Wind speeds, angular structure and gas temperatures are prescribed
Planck/grey atmospheres and approximate ionization and radiation transfer
No resolved 3D turbulence, shocks, knots, jets or binary shaping
The actual Sun may not form a bright planetary nebula

VISUALIZATION
Enlarged unresolved cells and a separate representative surface clock
Line-channel false colors and warm infrared dust colors
100× line sensitivity, separate stellar exposure and an optical halo
32 seconds compress billions of stellar years and ≈16,000 nebular years
Physics-informed numerical visualization
Not an observation or a fully resolved 3D radiation-hydrodynamic simulation
