`projects/sideratlas-planetary-nebula/vendor/no003/`. Its old pulsation physics,
mode data and DA atmosphere are not executed by this model.

redistribution permission is claimed. No raw solar cubes are included.

Latin Modern retains the GUST Font License in the project's fonts directory.

provider rights/citations; the MIT code licence is not a new licence for those

during movie rendering. Full scientific references are in docs/REFERENCES.md.
